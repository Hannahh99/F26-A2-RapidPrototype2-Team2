# F26-A2-RapidPrototype2-Team2
## Enemy Evade Prototype
2D Platformer — Game Design rapid prototype 2 team project.

Players must navigate through each level while avoiding the enemy whose movement is linked to their own. Moving in one direction causes the enemy to move in the opposite direction, and jumping causes the enemy to jump as well. Players will have to consider how their actions affect both characters to reach the end of each level.

### Controls:

- 'A' to move left
- 'D' to move right
- 'SPACEBAR' to jump
